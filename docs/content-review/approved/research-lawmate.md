# Lawmate: accessible legal aid

## English

**Eyebrow:** User-centred design · Group project · 2023  
**Subtitle:** Exploring how international residents in Ireland could understand legal information and reach credible, affordable support.  
**Tags:** Survey · Stakeholder interview · Information architecture · Concept testing

### Research question

How might we make Irish legal information easier to understand while preserving privacy and offering a path to credible, affordable support?

### Methods

The multidisciplinary student team used a survey, target-user research, a Legal Aid Board interview, personas, concept testing and heuristic evaluation. The team also completed an internal card sort to organise information and compared two early prototypes with two users.

### Key tensions

- Community participation can improve access to shared knowledge, but it introduces misinformation, harassment and privacy risks.
- Professional consultation offers more credible support, but affordability and availability remain barriers.
- Legal information needs to feel approachable without presenting an early design concept as legal advice.

### How the concept evolved

Testing showed that neither a community-only concept nor a consultation-only concept was sufficient. The final direction combined accessible legal resources, anonymous participation and a route to professional consultation.

### My contribution

I contributed research synthesis, information architecture and product-design collaboration within the multidisciplinary student team.

### Limitations

The prototype comparison involved two users, the card sort was conducted within the project team and several product areas remained incomplete. Lawmate is an early design direction, not a validated legal-service model or a source of legal advice.

## 中文

**标签：** 以用户为中心的设计 · 小组项目 · 2023  
**副标题：** 探索如何帮助居住在爱尔兰的国际人士理解法律信息，并获得可信且可负担的支持。  
**方法标签：** 问卷 · 利益相关方访谈 · 信息架构 · 概念测试

### 研究问题

我们如何在保护隐私的同时，让爱尔兰法律信息更容易理解，并为用户提供通往可信、可负担支持的路径？

### 研究方法

跨学科学生团队使用了问卷、目标用户研究、Legal Aid Board 访谈、用户画像、概念测试和启发式评估。团队还在内部完成卡片分类，并邀请两名用户比较两个早期原型。

### 核心矛盾

- 社区参与可以增加共享知识的可访问性，但也会引入错误信息、骚扰和隐私风险。
- 专业咨询更加可信，但价格和可获得性仍然是障碍。
- 法律信息需要易于理解，同时不能把早期设计概念包装成法律建议。

### 方案如何演进

测试表明，只依赖社区或只依赖专业咨询都不够。最终方向结合了易于理解的法律资源、匿名参与，以及连接专业咨询的路径。

### 我的贡献

我在跨学科学生团队中参与研究归纳、信息架构和产品设计协作。

### 局限

原型比较只有两名用户，卡片分类由项目团队内部完成，并且部分产品区域尚未完成。Lawmate 是早期设计方向，不是经过验证的法律服务，也不提供法律建议。
