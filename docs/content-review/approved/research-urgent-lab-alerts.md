# Designing a safer urgent lab alert workflow

## English

**Eyebrow:** Healthcare HCI · 2024  
**Subtitle:** Translating communication delays, security concerns and clinical accountability into a traceable alert workflow.  
**Tags:** Survey (n=9) · Expert interview · Workflow mapping · Prototyping

### Research question

How might we communicate urgent laboratory results securely and quickly while ensuring that the responsible clinical team acknowledges and acts on them?

### Methods

The project combined a literature review, a Google Forms survey of nine healthcare professionals and one expert interview with Dr Grace. Survey participants included five doctors, two laboratory staff and two consultant microbiologists. The team used the findings for workflow mapping, requirements definition and iterative prototyping.

### Findings

- Eight of nine survey participants identified communication delay as a major problem.
- Respondents also raised missed alerts, unclear responsibility, technical failure and information-security concerns.
- A notification alone was insufficient. The workflow needed acknowledgement, ownership, tracking and escalation.

### Design response

The concept used structured alert creation, urgency levels, secure delivery, acknowledgement and visible status. Ongoing and completed states made it possible to see whether the responsible team had received and acted on an alert.

### My contribution

I contributed product framing, research synthesis, requirements definition and prototype evaluation within a multidisciplinary HCI team.

### Limitations

This was an exploratory academic project with nine survey participants and one expert interview. It evaluated a prototype rather than a deployed clinical system. Integration, escalation rules and real-world response times require further clinical and technical validation.

## 中文

**标签：** 医疗 HCI · 2024  
**副标题：** 把沟通延迟、安全要求和临床责任转化为可追踪的紧急提醒工作流。  
**方法标签：** 9 人问卷 · 专家访谈 · 工作流梳理 · 原型设计

### 研究问题

我们如何安全、快速地传递紧急检验结果，同时确保负责的临床团队确认接收并采取行动？

### 研究方法

项目结合了文献综述、9 名医疗专业人士参与的 Google Forms 问卷，以及与 Dr Grace 的一场专家访谈。问卷参与者包括 5 名医生、2 名实验室工作人员和 2 名顾问微生物学家。团队把研究结果用于工作流梳理、需求定义和迭代原型设计。

### 主要发现

- 9 名问卷参与者中有 8 名将沟通延迟视为主要问题。
- 参与者还提到提醒遗漏、责任不清、技术故障和信息安全风险。
- 只发送通知并不够，工作流还需要确认、责任归属、追踪和升级机制。

### 设计回应

方案包含结构化提醒创建、紧急程度、安全传递、接收确认和可见状态。进行中与已完成状态让团队能够看到负责人员是否收到提醒并采取行动。

### 我的贡献

我在跨学科 HCI 团队中参与产品问题定义、研究归纳、需求定义和原型评估。

### 局限

这是一个探索性学术项目，样本为 9 名问卷参与者和 1 名专家访谈对象，评估的是原型而不是已经部署的临床系统。系统集成、升级规则和真实响应时间仍需要进一步的临床与技术验证。
