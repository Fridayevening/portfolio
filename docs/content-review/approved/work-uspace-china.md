# Uspace China market platform

## English

**Kicker:** China localisation · Enterprise platform  
**Title:** Adapting a connected enterprise platform for China  
**Summary:** I led the China-market adaptation of established international product patterns. Instead of translating the existing apps, the team rebuilt key mobile workflows around WeChat and expanded the platform through customer-backed enterprise projects.

### Localisation beyond language

The international apps relied on native mobile distribution and email-based access. For China, the product moved essential UstarMobile and UstarAccess workflows into a WeChat Mini Program. Representative workflows included check-in, attendance, access requests and remote opening, meeting booking, notifications, directory access and account controls.

The platform also supported GPS and Wi-Fi based check-in and WeChat-oriented authentication. These changes reduced the need for employees to install and learn a separate enterprise app.

### Managing a broad administration platform

The administration side covered personnel, devices, authorisation, access records, holidays, access control, attendance, reports, visitors, permissions, meetings, approvals, integrations and operation logs. I managed priorities across these areas while delivering six major platform releases.

### Henan smart-campus delivery

The Henan Yuzi smart-campus project involved the education bureau, schools, hardware vendors, software integrators, telecom operators and Uniubi's internal team. I coordinated the six parties, separated core delivery from later improvements and used regular progress alignment to manage dependencies. The core project was delivered on time in three months, and the delivery model was later reused in other regions.

### Turning custom work into reusable product

XIZI Elevator required an elevator-control capability that was not yet part of the standard platform. The project created a working path for that requirement, and the capability later entered the standard Uspace product.

### What I learned

Localisation is a product decision, not a translation task. The useful question was not how to reproduce the international interface, but which workflows should stay, which interaction patterns needed to change and which customer-specific capabilities were reusable enough to become product.

## 中文

**标签：** 中国市场本地化 · 企业平台  
**标题：** 为中国市场重新设计互联企业平台  
**摘要：** 我负责把已经在国际市场验证过的产品模式适配到中国。团队没有简单翻译原有 App，而是围绕微信重建关键移动工作流，并通过真实企业项目扩展平台能力。

### 本地化不只是翻译

国际产品主要依赖原生 App 分发和邮箱登录。在中国，产品把 UstarMobile 和 UstarAccess 的关键工作流迁移到微信小程序，包括打卡、考勤、门禁权限申请和远程开门、会议预约、通知、通讯录和账户管理。

平台同时支持基于 GPS 与 Wi-Fi 的打卡，以及适合微信生态的认证方式，减少员工额外安装和学习独立企业 App 的成本。

### 管理广泛的平台能力

管理后台覆盖人员、设备、授权、出入记录、节假日、门禁、考勤、报表、访客、权限、会议、审批、平台集成和操作日志。我需要在这些能力之间管理优先级，并推动 6 个主要平台版本交付。

### 河南智慧校园交付

河南豫资智慧校园项目涉及教育局、学校、硬件供应商、软件集成商、运营商和宇泛内部团队。我负责协调六方，把核心交付与后续改进拆开，并通过固定的进度同步管理依赖。核心项目在三个月内按时交付，相关交付模式后来也复用到了其他地区。

### 把定制项目沉淀成产品能力

西子电梯需要当时标准平台尚未具备的电梯控制能力。项目先形成了可用方案，相关能力后来进入 Uspace 标准产品。

### 我的收获

本地化是一项产品决策，而不是翻译任务。真正的问题不是怎样复制国际版界面，而是哪些工作流应该保留、哪些交互方式必须改变，以及哪些客户定制能力具有足够复用价值，可以沉淀为标准产品。
