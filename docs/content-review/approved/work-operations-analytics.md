# Operations analytics and supply-chain visibility

## English

**Kicker:** Internal tools · Operations analytics  
**Title:** Turning scattered hardware operations data into a shared decision system  
**Summary:** While managing software products at Uniubi, I saw that hardware delivery and quality information was fragmented across systems and teams. I proposed and drove an operations-visibility system alongside my core product work.

### The problem

Product catalogue data, supplier information, delivery progress and component-quality signals existed, but teams did not have one shared view. That made it difficult to identify delivery risks early or connect hardware issues with the customer experience.

### What I built

The system combined:

- SKU and product-catalogue data;
- supplier information;
- order and delivery-stage tracking;
- component and supplier defect-rate monitoring;
- material-shortage alerts;
- Tableau dashboards for operational review;
- links between hardware fault or quality signals and software-usage or customer-support data.

### How it changed the work

The shared view gave product and operations teams a common way to examine delivery and quality risks. Supplier discussions could refer to observed trends, while product teams could investigate whether hardware issues were associated with different software behaviour or support demand.

### What I learned

Some valuable product opportunities sit outside the formal roadmap. This work started because I noticed a recurring operational blind spot and proposed a system that joined information already available across the business. The value came from connecting those signals, not from adding another isolated dashboard.

## 中文

**标签：** 内部工具 · 运营分析  
**标题：** 把分散的硬件运营数据变成共享决策系统  
**摘要：** 在宇泛负责软件产品期间，我发现硬件交付和质量信息分散在不同系统与团队中。因此，我在核心产品工作之外主动提出并推动建设运营可视化系统。

### 问题

产品目录、供应商、交付进度和部件质量信号都真实存在，但团队缺少统一视图。这使大家很难提前识别交付风险，也难以把硬件问题与客户的软件体验联系起来。

### 我建设的系统

系统整合了：

- SKU 和产品目录数据；
- 供应商信息；
- 订单和交付阶段追踪；
- 部件与供应商缺陷率监控；
- 物料短缺预警；
- 用于运营复盘的 Tableau 看板；
- 硬件故障或质量信号与软件使用、客户支持数据之间的关联。

### 它如何改变工作方式

统一视图让产品和运营团队可以用相同的信息讨论交付与质量风险。供应商沟通能够基于观察到的趋势，产品团队也可以进一步判断硬件问题是否与不同的软件使用行为或客服需求相关。

### 我的收获

一些有价值的产品机会并不在正式 Roadmap 中。这个项目来自我对重复运营盲区的观察，我推动的不是另一个孤立看板，而是把企业内部已经存在、却彼此分散的信号连接起来。
