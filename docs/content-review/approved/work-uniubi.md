# Uniubi connected enterprise portfolio

## English

**Kicker:** B2B SaaS · IoT · International product portfolio  
**Title:** Connecting devices, cloud software and mobile workflows across 16 countries  
**Summary:** I led product work across Uspace, a connected enterprise portfolio spanning Ustar, UstarCloud, UstarMobile and UstarAccess. The portfolio cumulatively served 2,409 enterprise organisations and 29K monthly active users across 16 countries.

**Metrics**

- 2,409 enterprise organisations, cumulative portfolio scope
- 29K monthly active users, full Uspace portfolio
- 16 countries
- 6 major platform releases

### From an offline utility to a product portfolio

Ustar began as an offline Windows client for organisations that needed to manage recognition devices, personnel, access control and attendance on a local network. UstarCloud extended those workflows into browser-based administration. UstarMobile gave employees access to check-in, attendance history, visitor notifications and company information, while UstarAccess added remote opening, access requests and meeting workflows for users who needed more control on mobile.

My role covered product direction, requirements, backlog decisions and cross-functional delivery. I worked with a fixed ten-person team and could bring in engineering capacity from other project teams when urgent iterations required it.

### Building for real operating conditions

Many customers operated across multiple sites and did not have reliable connectivity everywhere. Offline support was therefore a core product condition rather than an edge case. The product family separated local device operations, cloud administration and mobile workflows so that each user could complete the work appropriate to their environment.

### Turning compliance into product architecture

European expansion exposed a risk in the original raw-face-image storage approach. I worked with the team to move the product towards feature-value processing, completed the architecture change within one month, and documented the relevant requirements in a reusable compliance template for later international product lines.

### Responding to an unexpected market signal

During COVID, customers began asking whether access devices could also support temperature screening. I built the business case and coordinated hardware, software and algorithm teams. The product launched within six months and helped the company open opportunities in Africa and Latin America.

### Pricing around customer use

The original flat-fee structure did not reflect differences in customer scale or product use. I helped redesign pricing around devices, users and feature modules. The change contributed to 15% year-on-year revenue growth.

### What I learned

The strongest product strategy was not a single feature list. It was the sequence: solve local device operations, add cloud administration, extend essential workflows to employees, and then give managers higher-control mobile tools. The same principle shaped compliance and pricing work: understand the operating constraint first, then change the product system around it.

### 从离线工具到完整产品组合

Ustar 最初是离线 Windows 客户端，帮助企业在局域网内管理识别设备、人员、门禁和考勤。UstarCloud 把这些能力扩展到浏览器端管理；UstarMobile 为员工提供打卡、考勤历史、访客通知和企业信息；UstarAccess 则进一步支持远程开门、权限申请和会议等需要更高控制权限的移动工作流。

我的职责覆盖产品方向、需求、Backlog 决策和跨团队交付。我与固定的 10 人团队协作，在紧急迭代时也可以从其他项目组协调研发资源。

### 围绕真实环境设计

很多客户跨地点运营，部分环境的网络并不稳定。因此，离线能力不是边缘需求，而是产品基础条件。整个产品组合把本地设备操作、云端管理和移动工作流拆开，让不同角色在各自环境里完成合适的任务。

### 把合规要求转化为产品架构

进入欧洲市场时，原有的原始人脸图像存储方式带来了合规风险。我与团队推动产品转向特征值处理，在一个月内完成架构调整，并将相关要求整理成可供后续国际产品线复用的合规模板。

### 响应计划之外的市场信号

疫情期间，客户开始询问门禁设备能否同时支持测温。我提出商业案例，协调硬件、软件和算法团队，在六个月内完成产品上市，并帮助公司拓展非洲和拉丁美洲市场机会。

### 让定价贴近实际使用

原有统一收费方式无法反映客户规模和使用差异。我参与将定价调整为设备数、用户数和功能模块三个维度。这次调整为产品线 15% 的年度收入增长作出了贡献。

### 我的收获

这段经历让我理解，产品策略不是一张功能清单，而是正确的建设顺序：先解决本地设备管理，再建立云端管理，把必要工作流延伸给员工，最后为管理者提供更高权限的移动能力。合规和定价也是同样的逻辑：先理解真实约束，再围绕约束调整整个产品系统。
