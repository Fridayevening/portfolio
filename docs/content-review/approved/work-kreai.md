# KreAI creator business platform

## English

**Kicker:** AI SaaS · 0→1  
**Title:** Designing an AI negotiation workflow, then learning where automation should stop  
**Summary:** As the sole product lead in a seven-person cross-functional team, I turned an early idea into six product versions in six months. KreAI helped creators identify brand opportunities and manage collaboration emails through a five-stage workflow.

**Metrics**

- 6 versions in 6 months
- 7-person cross-functional team
- 5 workflow stages
- 10-customer directional comparison

### The product

KreAI connected to a creator's mailbox, identified potential brand collaborations and organised them into five stages: Initial Outreach, Content Alignment, Price Negotiation, Contract Review and Deal Confirmation. The product also included opportunity discovery, creator profiles, task generation and tiered subscription flows with Stripe integration.

### Finding the boundary of automation

The MVP automated all five stages. Early research used questionnaires, observation and, with permission, review of emails sent by the system. Of ten early testers, eight encountered problems during pricing or contract work. Formula-based pricing was too rigid, while contract generation moved between unsupported clauses and overly fixed templates.

I changed pricing and contract review to an AI-draft plus human-confirmation model. Initial outreach, content alignment and deal confirmation could remain automated, while users retained control over money and contractual commitments.

### Measuring the redesign carefully

Ten sample customers each used two versions on two weeks of their own mailbox data. Product event tracking and manual records showed full five-stage completion moving from approximately 30% to 38%, a relative improvement of approximately 25%.

This was a small, non-randomised directional comparison. It indicated that the redesign was worth continuing, but it was not a causal or population-level result.

### The business lesson

The product reached a working state, but customer acquisition did not extend beyond approximately 100 seed users from the founder's network. The project later ended amid acquisition and fundraising constraints. The experience changed how I approach 0→1 work: distribution and repeated user acquisition need validation alongside the product, not after it.

## 中文

**标签：** AI SaaS · 0→1  
**标题：** 设计 AI 谈判工作流，并找到自动化应该停止的位置  
**摘要：** 作为 7 人跨职能团队中唯一的产品负责人，我在 6 个月内把早期想法推进为 6 个产品版本。KreAI 帮助创作者发现品牌合作机会，并通过五阶段工作流管理商务邮件。

**关键数字**

- 6 个月交付 6 个版本
- 7 人跨职能团队
- 5 个工作流阶段
- 10 个样本客户的方向性比较

### 产品

KreAI 连接创作者邮箱，识别潜在品牌合作，并把沟通组织为五个阶段：初次联系、内容对齐、价格谈判、合同审核和确认合作。产品还包括商机发现、创作者资料、任务生成，以及接入 Stripe 的分层订阅流程。

### 找到自动化边界

MVP 最初自动执行全部五个阶段。早期研究包括问卷、观察，以及在获得用户允许后查看系统实际发出的邮件。10 名早期测试用户中，有 8 人在价格或合同阶段遇到问题。公式化报价过于僵硬；合同生成则会在无依据的条款和过度固定的模板之间摇摆。

我将价格谈判和合同审核改为“AI 起草 + 人工确认”。初次联系、内容对齐和确认合作可以继续自动化，而涉及金额与合同责任的部分由用户保留最终控制权。

### 谨慎衡量改版结果

10 个样本客户分别使用两个版本处理各两周自己的邮箱数据。产品埋点与人工记录显示，完整走完五阶段的比例约从 30% 提升到 38%，相对提升约 25%。

这是一个小样本、非随机的方向性比较。它说明改版值得继续验证，但不能被解释为因果结论或整体用户结果。

### 商业层面的教训

产品达到了可运行状态，但获客没有突破创始人人脉带来的约 100 个种子用户。项目后来因获客和融资限制而结束。这段经历改变了我对 0→1 的理解：分发与持续获客必须和产品一起验证，而不是等产品建完后再开始。
